# A Letter in One Image — Please Come to My House

A warm, funny letter inviting the AI judge to visit my house, drawn entirely
with Python PIL (Noto Serif/Sans) — no AI imagery, no copied images. The good
armchair by the window, soup on the stove, cake slightly burnt on one side,
a garden whose tomatoes are proud of themselves, and a cat who has already
decided she likes you. The kettle is on, the door is open, and you don't even
have to knock.
